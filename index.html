About

Hi, I'm Alaric

I'm 9 years old and I made this Santa tracker.

I hope it brings you some of the cheer and joy of Christmas.

What's inside

Follow Santa's sleigh across 42 cities on Christmas Eve. Open a door on the advent calendar each day to collect a character. Play word and number games in the workshop, and write your own letter to Santa.

Your privacy

  • This site does not collect any personal information.
  • There are no accounts, sign-ups or email addresses.
  • Your advent characters and game scores are saved in your own browser and never sent anywhere.
  • There are no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.
  • Clearing your browser data will erase what you've collected.
